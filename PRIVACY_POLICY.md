# Privacy Policy for Beacon App

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-29

## 1. Introduction

Beacon Children's Center ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Beacon App (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information and the sensitive health data of your children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services (collectively, the "Services").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Services:

### A. Personal Information (Guardians/Parents)
*   **Identity Data:** Name, username.
*   **Contact Data:** Email address, phone number.
*   **Authentication Data:** Passwords and login credentials (stored securely).

### B. Patient & Child Data (Protected Health Information)
*   **Identity Data:** Child's name, date of birth, gender, and relationship to the guardian.
*   **Health Records:** Medical history, growth measurements (height, weight, etc.), developmental milestones, prescriptions, and diagnosis information.
*   **Appointment Data:** History of visits, scheduled appointments, and doctor interactions.

### C. Device and Technical Data
*   **Device Permissions:**
    *   **Camera & Microphone:** Used for video consultations and uploading profile images.
    *   **Notifications:** Used for appointment reminders and health alerts.
    *   **Storage/Media:** Access to upload medical reports or relevant images.
*   **Usage Data:** Logs, error reports, and interaction data to help us improve app performance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:
*   **Service Delivery:** To facilitate appointment bookings, telemedicine consultations, and child development tracking.
*   **Medical Care:** To enable our doctors and healthcare providers to view patient history and provide accurate medical advice.
*   **Communication:** To send appointment reminders and important updates about our services.
*   **Improvement:** To analyze usage trends and enhance the user experience of the App.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information in the following strictly limited circumstances:
*   **Healthcare Providers:** With doctors and medical staff at Beacon Children's Center to provide medical care.
*   **Service Providers:** With trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating the App (e.g., cloud hosting, SMS services), bound by confidentiality agreements.
*   **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal and medical data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by medical record-keeping laws and regulations in Kenya.

## 6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data, including:
*   Encryption of data in transit and at rest.
*   Secure authentication mechanisms.
*   Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

While our App tracks children's health, it is intended to be used by parents or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 18 without parental consent.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
